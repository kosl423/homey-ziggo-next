After starting or reconnecting, Connected does not yet mean live power status is known. If the device warns that it is waiting for live status, switch the Mini once with the Ziggo remote and wait until Homey follows. A saved status alone never authorizes a Power command.

Diagnostic log

Routine sign-in checks are summarized per ten minutes, with UTC start/end times and counts. Unexpected errors and Power events remain separate. The newest 250 entries are retained; a busy log can still cover less than a full night.

Clear the ordinary diagnostic log. New events may appear afterward. MQTT history and sign-in data are kept.

MQTT connection history

The newest 50 connection events across all devices are saved, oldest first, with UTC timestamps. They survive app restarts independently of the 250-line diagnostic log. New events replace the oldest; there is no expiry by age. Frequent failures can exhaust the history before a full night has passed.

Technical event details

Run distinguishes app starts; device is an anonymous number within a run. Monitor and generation distinguish connections. Attempt counts monitor starts and reconnect attempts since the last ready. Ready/restored means the subscription is ready; live power status may still be unknown. Restoration is recorded within the same device lifecycle.

History refresh and copy only read data. Clear diagnostics does not clear MQTT history. There is no MQTT reset button. Removing the app removes its settings. A crash or storage error can lose recent events. MQTT history contains no tokens, cookies, device IDs, topics, raw error text or payloads.
