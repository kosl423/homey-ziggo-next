## Device product images

The device images use the user-supplied photograph `IMG_7724.jpg`, selected by the project owner for this local candidate. The image was edited using the built-in image generation tool to isolate the device on white, remove the surrounding cabinet, receiver and cables, adjust lighting and retouch dust. Only the supplied photograph was used as an input to this edit; the earlier official Ziggo product image was not used. This is an AI-assisted photographic edit, not an untouched photograph or a guarantee of exact pixel-level fidelity. Review small details against the source and physical device before submission.

The selected square edit is proportionally resized to the three device formats. It replaces the earlier official Ziggo product artwork at the existing driver manifest paths. The source photograph and edit prompt are retained privately outside this repository. Original photo metadata is not included in the distributed exports.

Prepared device exports: 75×75, 500×500 and 1000×1000 pixels, under `drivers/ziggo_next/assets/images/`.

Device PNG exports are opaque and have no source metadata copied into them.

On November 13, 2026, the project owner confirmed personally taking IMG_7724.jpg and permitted its use for the Homey App Store, including the selected edited product images. The original declaration is retained in the local submission dossier outside the app package. This permission records the planned App Store use; it does not transfer copyright, grant a blanket MIT license or authorise unrelated uses.

## Icons

- `assets/icon.svg`: original television/play/connection line drawing (concept A), selected by the project owner. Black strokes on a transparent 960×960 canvas, with no embedded bitmap, font or official Ziggo wordmark paths. This project-created icon is covered by the project's MIT license. Concept source and previews are retained in the local preparation evidence under `app-icon-concepts/`.
- `drivers/ziggo_next/assets/icon.svg`: original near-top-view outline drawing of the Next Mini, replacing the earlier edge-on perspective at the owner's request. The broad rounded body, inset top, shallow front edge and power button are simplified for recognition at small sizes. The small fabric tab contains a plain path-drawn letter Z, not the official Ziggo emblem. It is distinct from the television app icon, uses a transparent 960×960 canvas and contains no embedded bitmap, font, gradient or background. The top-view concept and supplied device photograph informed the general form; no official logo paths are copied. It is a simplified illustration, not a technical drawing or an exact photo trace.

No Homey logo, Homey device or Homey interface screenshot is included in the image assets. The existing application code and device identity are unchanged by these visual changes.
